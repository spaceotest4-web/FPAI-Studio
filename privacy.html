Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aichance ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at www.aichance.org and the Aichance Android application (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on how you use the Service, this may include:

  • Usage data — anonymous, aggregated information such as pages visited, browser type, device type, and general location (country level), collected through standard web logs and analytics.
  • Prompts and generated content — text you enter to generate images or video is processed to produce your result. We do not require an account to use the web generator.
  • Information you provide — if you contact us, we receive the email address and message you send.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, understand usage, and (where applicable) serve advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect how the site works.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit the Service. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Third-party vendors and ad networks may serve content and advertisements and collect information through this site; their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the Service in aggregate. These services may set cookies and collect usage data as described in their own privacy policies.

How we use information

  • To provide and operate the Service and generate the content you request.
  • To maintain, improve, and secure the Service.
  • To display advertising that helps keep the Service free.
  • To respond to your enquiries.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers (such as advertising, hosting, and analytics partners) who process it on our behalf, or where required by law.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. You can manage advertising cookies through the links above and control cookies in your browser. To exercise other rights, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do, and significant changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us via our Contact page or email support@aichance.org.
