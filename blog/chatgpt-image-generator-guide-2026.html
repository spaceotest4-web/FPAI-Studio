Typing a sentence into ChatGPT and getting a finished image back is, for many people, the first time AI image generation ever "clicked". It is built right into the chat, understands messy natural language, and lets you refine results by just talking. This guide covers how to use the ChatGPT image generator, what the free plan actually allows, what it is genuinely good at, and the best free alternative for when you hit its limits or want AI video too.

The short version: ChatGPT's image generator (OpenAI's GPT Image, the successor to DALL-E) is excellent at conversational prompts and text-in-image, free with daily caps and an OpenAI account. For no-account access, no daily cap on a free plan, or AI video, Aichance's free generator is the best companion.

What is the ChatGPT image generator?

The ChatGPT image generator is OpenAI's native image model built into ChatGPT. You do not open a separate tool — you describe an image inside a normal chat and it appears inline. Under the hood it is OpenAI's current image model (the successor to DALL-E, commonly called GPT Image), and it handles three jobs: generating images from text, editing or restyling an image you upload, and iterating through follow-up messages while keeping the conversation's context.

Because it lives inside ChatGPT, it inherits the chat's biggest strength: it understands plain, conversational language. You do not need prompt-engineering skill to get a usable first result — though a little still helps.

How to generate images in ChatGPT

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT (web or the mobile app) with your OpenAI account.
  2. Start a chat and describe the image — e.g., "create an image of a modern kitchen with marble counters, bright daylight".
  3. ChatGPT generates the image inline in the conversation.
  4. Refine with follow-ups — "make it evening", "add a window on the left", "more minimalist".
  5. To edit your own picture, upload it and describe the change in plain language.
  6. Download the result when you are happy with it.

Free vs paid: the real limits

The ChatGPT image generator is available on free accounts, but with a daily generation cap — fine for occasional images, limiting for a heavy session. Paid tiers (ChatGPT Plus and Pro) raise the limits and generation speed. Two things are constant on every tier: you need an OpenAI account, and the image flow focuses on stills, not video.

As with every AI service, exact caps shift over time, so treat the daily free allowance as "enough to try, not enough to rely on" for high-volume work.

What it does well

  • Conversational prompts — messy, natural descriptions work without prompt skill.
  • Text inside images — legible words on signs, posters, and mock-ups are a relative strength.
  • Iterative editing — refine by chatting, with context carried between messages.
  • One place — no separate app; it is inside a tool millions already use daily.

Where it falls short

  • An OpenAI account is required — no instant, sign-in-free generation.
  • Daily caps on the free plan — heavy use pushes you toward a paid tier.
  • No AI video in the standard in-chat image flow — that is a separate OpenAI product.
  • No model choice or seed control — you get one pipeline, not a lineup of image models with repeatability controls.

FAQ

How do I generate images in ChatGPT?

Sign in to ChatGPT, start a chat, and simply describe the image you want — for example, "create an image of a mountain cabin at sunset". ChatGPT generates the picture inline, and you can refine it with follow-up messages like "make it winter" or "add a car in the driveway". You can also upload an image and ask it to edit or restyle it.

Is the ChatGPT image generator free?

Free ChatGPT accounts can generate a limited number of images per day. Heavier use, faster generation, and higher limits require a paid plan such as ChatGPT Plus or Pro. So image generation is available for free but capped, and it requires an OpenAI account.

What model powers ChatGPT image generation?

ChatGPT generates images with OpenAI's built-in image model (the successor to DALL-E, often referred to as GPT Image). It is strong at understanding natural, conversational prompts and at rendering legible text inside images, and it edits uploaded pictures through plain-language instructions.

Can ChatGPT generate videos?

The core ChatGPT image generator produces still images. OpenAI's video generation is a separate product, not part of the standard in-chat image flow. If you want image and video in one free tool, Aichance includes ten AI video models alongside its image generation.
